# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-22)

- First version: three measured scientific outcomes from three sources.
- Bell (1964): a local hidden-variable model (Monte Carlo, 1,000,000
  samples per correlation, frozen seeds {42, 7, 1234, 2026, 2718}) stays
  at CHSH |S| <= 2 while the quantum singlet reaches 2 sqrt(2); at
  equal settings the outcomes are perfectly anticorrelated.
- Inertia: the disk-slice integral of a solid sphere (Simpson's rule)
  gives (2/5) M R^2, and the sphere carries 3/5 of a thin shell's
  inertia.
- Zeta (Riemann, 1859): the first ten zeros on the critical line,
  computed from scratch by Euler-Maclaurin summation (N = 100, K = 6),
  land on the established values to 4.78e-07; ten zeros sit below
  t = 50, against a Riemann-von Mangoldt estimate of 9.42.
- Measured claims (bars frozen before evaluation): **7/7 pass, 23/23
  tests pass.**
